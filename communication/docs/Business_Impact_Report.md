# Business Impact Report

Purpose

Summarize the expected business value, cost/benefit estimates, and quantifiable impacts of implementing the recommendations from the forecasting and analysis work.

Summary metrics

- Estimated incremental revenue uplift from dynamic pricing: 3–5% annually
- Cost savings from improved inventory planning: 2–4% of COGS
- Time-to-insight: from raw data to dashboard < 1 day with automation

Assumptions

- Models retrained monthly with up-to-date data
- Price elasticity consistent with historical measurement
- No major structural shock to the market during the next 12 months

ROI Estimate (illustrative)

- Baseline revenue: $100M
- Revenue uplift (3%): $3M incremental revenue
- Implementation cost: $50k–$150k (data engineering + dashboarding)
- Payback period: < 12 months in expected-case scenario

Risks & Mitigations

- Data gaps (GDP/inflation): prioritize sourcing external data and automating ingestion.
- Model degradation: set up monitoring and retraining triggers.
