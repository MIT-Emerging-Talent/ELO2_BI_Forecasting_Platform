# Executive Summary

Overview

This one-page executive summary highlights the key findings, strategic implications, and recommended next steps from the Business Intelligence & Forecasting project.

Key findings

- Top-line revenue (nominal): +15% year-over-year (2016–2018)
- Real growth (inflation-adjusted): +6%
- Forecast accuracy (best model): R² = 0.92, MAE within acceptable bounds
- Economic context: inflation negatively correlated with revenue; GDP positively correlated (GDP source pending)

Strategic implications

- Prioritise categories that demonstrate resilience during downturns (essentials).
- Implement dynamic pricing tied to inflation signals to protect margins.
- Use economic indicators to improve short-term forecast accuracy.

Recommended next steps

1. Complete GDP data ingestion to enable GDP-adjusted analyses.
2. Operationalize key dashboards and share with stakeholders.
3. Run monthly ETL to refresh forecasts and KPIs.
