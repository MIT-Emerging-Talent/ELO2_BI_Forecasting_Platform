# Stakeholder Engagement Plan

Objective

Define stakeholders and how we'll engage them during rollout.

Stakeholders

- CEO / Executive team — strategic decisions
- Finance — forecast validation and budget alignment
- Sales / Ops — operational inputs and feedback
- IT — deployment & access

Engagement activities

- Kickoff meeting
- Regular demo sessions
- Feedback collection form

Success criteria

- Stakeholder sign-off on KPIs
- Adoption of dashboard in monthly reviews
