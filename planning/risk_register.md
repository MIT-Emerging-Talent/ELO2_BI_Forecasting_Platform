# Risk Register

Identify risks and mitigations for project delivery.

1. Data availability — missing GDP/inflation
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: Medium
- Mitigation: Add fallback external sources and document gaps.

2. Model drift
- Likelihood: Medium
- Impact: High
- Mitigation: Monitoring & automated retraining triggers

3. Adoption
- Likelihood: Low
- Impact: Medium
- Mitigation: Stakeholder engagement and training

Ownership

- Each risk should have a named owner and an action plan.
